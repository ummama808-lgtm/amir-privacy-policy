Privacy Policy

AI Dropshipping Automation

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Dropshipping Automation (the “Application”) handles information when an authorized seller connects an eBay account and uses the Application to manage product and listing workflows.

Information the Application may process

  • eBay account and authorization information needed to connect to eBay and perform actions the seller requests.
  • Product, inventory, offer, listing, pricing, shipping-policy, and order-related information made available through the eBay APIs or entered by the seller.
  • Supplier and product information obtained from connected supplier services, when the seller uses those features.
  • Technical logs and error details needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Application.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide and maintain the Application's seller-authorized functions, including connecting to eBay, preparing or managing product listings, checking listing and inventory information, troubleshooting errors, and protecting the Application from misuse.

eBay data and authorization

The Application accesses eBay information only after the seller authorizes it and only to provide the functions requested by that seller. Sellers can revoke the Application's access through their eBay account settings. The Application's use of eBay API data is subject to the applicable eBay terms and policies.

Sharing

The Application does not sell personal information. Information may be sent to eBay and to supplier or infrastructure services only as needed to carry out the seller-requested workflow, operate the Application, or comply with applicable law. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. Information is kept only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, to maintain workflow records, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may stop using the Application and revoke its eBay authorization. To ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of information handled by the Application, contact the operator using the email address below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and recordkeeping purposes.

Children

The Application is intended for business sellers and is not directed to children.
